The Origin

Founded in 2010, Stackly began with a simple belief: the process of buying or selling a home shouldn't be the most stressful event of your life. We started as a small boutique agency in the downtown district, committed to stripping away the confusing jargon and hidden fees that plagued the industry.

Our Approach

Today, we pair decades of hyper-local neighborhood expertise with cutting-edge market analysis technology. Whether you are searching for a high-rise luxury penthouse or a quiet suburban starter home, our agents provide complete transparency, aggressive negotiation, and a seamless digital closing process.
